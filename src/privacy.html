This website

This website does not set advertising cookies and does not use analytics that follow you around the web. Fonts are loaded from Google Fonts, which may log your IP address. If I add analytics later, I'll use a privacy-friendly tool and update this page.

Messages you send

If you use the contact form or email me, I receive your name, email and message. This isn't a secure channel. Please don't send clinical details, and please don't send anything urgent. I use your message only to reply to you. I don't sell or share it, and I don't add you to a mailing list unless you ask.

My contact form is processed by a third-party form service so I can receive it. Scheduling is handled by a third-party scheduling service. Neither is used to store clinical information.

If you become a client

Clinical records, intake forms, notes, billing and video sessions are kept in a secure system designed for health information under HIPAA, with a signed business associate agreement. You'll receive a Notice of Privacy Practices at intake explaining how your information is used, your rights, and the limits of confidentiality.
