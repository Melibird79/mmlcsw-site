You might recognize this

You've been good at this for a long time.

You think fast, you read the room before anyone says a word, and you've made yourself useful to everyone. It worked, mostly. Then something starts to give. Sleep. A relationship. Your temper. The loop at 3 a.m. The way your body checks out of a conversation the moment it gets loud.

You can usually name exactly what's happening, and you still can't think your way out of it. That tends to mean the thinking part of you has done all it can, and the rest of the work lives in the body.

One honest note: I'm not a performance coach. I can't help you get better at the hamster wheel when you're already exhausted from running it. I can help you find out what's under the performing and what you actually want.

How I work

Beyond talk.

Talking helps, and there's also work that words don't reach. I'm trained in EMDR and the Flash Technique, and I use parts work and body-based practices alongside conversation, so the work includes your body and nervous system, not only your thoughts.

Every client leaves with one small practice, five minutes or less, to repeat daily. I'd rather you have a short thing you actually do than a long list you don't.

Who tends to do well here

  • Capable people who have always been the one holding it together, in any line of work
  • People at a turning point: burnout that won't lift, a career or relationship ending, a late diagnosis
  • Gifted and twice-exceptional adults, including late-identified ADHD and autism, who have been masking for years
  • Therapists and other helpers who need a place to be the client
  • People with a trauma history that gets louder when the pressure goes up

If you're in active crisis, or need something I can't safely offer, I'll help you find the right level of care.
